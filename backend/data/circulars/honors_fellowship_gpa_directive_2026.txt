ACADEMIC REGULATION 2026: HONORS & FELLOWSHIP ELIGIBILITY DIRECTIVE
Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs
Published: February 10, 2026
Document ID: DOC-HONORS-2026-02

1.0 OVERVIEW
This directive sets forth updated standards for undergraduate honors designation and scholarship fellowship continuation.

2.0 APPLICABILITY & SCOPE
This regulation applies to all registered students in undergraduate engineering semester S5 across all departments.

3.0 ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE CRITERIA
Section 2.4 Merit Benchmark: Commencing Academic Year 2026-27, the minimum cumulative grade point average (GPA) required for continuing academic honors and tuition fellowship has been revised from 3.2 to 3.5.
Section 2.5: Students whose cumulative GPA falls below 3.5 will be flagged for probationary review.

4.0 EFFECTIVE DATE
Effective immediately for the Spring 2026 term.
