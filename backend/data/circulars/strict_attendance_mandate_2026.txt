ACADEMIC REGULATION 2026: STRICT LABORATORY & LECTURE ATTENDANCE MANDATE
Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs
Published: March 1, 2026
Document ID: DOC-ATTEND-STRICT-2026

1.0 PURPOSE & OVERVIEW
This directive raises the compliance threshold for high-intensity laboratory and core engineering courses.

2.0 SCOPE OF APPLICATION
This directive applies strictly to all S5 undergraduate courses across Computer Science and Electrical Engineering.

3.0 MANDATORY THRESHOLD
Section 4.1 Strict Attendance: The minimum lecture and practical attendance requirement for examination clearance has been revised from 80% to 85%.
Section 4.2: Any student recording attendance below 85% at the conclusion of instruction will be automatically denied end-semester examination hall tickets.

4.0 TIMELINE
Enforced immediately for the academic session 2026-27.
