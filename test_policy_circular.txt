Date: September 2026

SUBJECT: Revised Minimum Attendance Requirement for Examination Eligibility

1. BACKGROUND
Following the Academic Standards Review conducted in August 2026, the Board of
Academic Affairs has approved a revision to the minimum attendance threshold
required for students to appear in semester-end examinations.

2. REVISED POLICY (Section 3.2)
With immediate effect from the current semester, the minimum attendance
requirement is revised from 75% to 80% of total classes conducted per subject.

Students failing to meet the 80% attendance threshold shall be deemed INELIGIBLE
to appear in the semester-end examination for that subject.

3. SCOPE
This policy applies to:
- All undergraduate students (Semesters 1 through 8)
- All postgraduate students (Semesters 1 through 4)
- All departments across the institution

4. CALCULATION METHOD
Attendance percentage = (Classes attended / Total classes conducted) x 100
The threshold of 80.0% must be met individually for each enrolled subject.

5. EFFECTIVE DATE
This revised threshold (80%) replaces the previous requirement (75%) and is
effective from the academic year 2026-27, Semester 1 onwards.
